01About

Hi, I am Bisrat.

I am a senior at UNLV in the Howard R. Hughes College of Engineering, majoring in Computer Science with a minor in Information Systems. I graduate in May 2027.

I am of Ethiopian heritage and a first generation college student. I am a builder at heart. I like building things for problems I'd like to get solved.

My work lives at the crossing of movement, media, and software: competitive dance, photo and film, and the things I build in the open.

02Dance

I care about
my process.

I picked up dancing later in life, so everything I have in movement I earned through reps. I danced with the UNLV Rebel Girls and Company from April 2023 to May 2026. We became 3x national champions, and I won gold with Team USA at the ICU World Championship in 2025.

Before college I danced with the Diamondback Dance Crew at Desert Oasis High School, where we won the NDA national title in Medium Varsity Hip Hop in 2023.

I teach dance too. From November 2024 to March 2026 I was a dance instructor at Captivation Dance Affiliates, choreographing weekly for kids from kindergarten through 12th grade.

This winter I am doing a 7 week solo intensive, relearning championship routines from the best crews in the world and closing with my own team's routines.
